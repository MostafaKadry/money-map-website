What {{site.name}} does

A mobile-first tracker for people who want a clear view of their money without handing it to a stranger.

📈

Net worth at a glance

Assets minus liabilities, with a monthly income and expense summary. Balances are calculated from your transactions.

💵

Income, expenses, transfers

Record every kind of movement. Transfers into a liability reduce debt, and cross-currency transfers keep the amount received.

🌎

Multiple currencies

Each account has its own currency. Totals convert to the currency you choose, using cached rates that also work offline.

🎯

Budgets and goals

Set monthly budgets and savings goals, and see progress. Recurring transactions are posted for you when you open the app.

🪙

Gold holdings

Track gold by karat and weight and include its value in your net worth, using a market quote or your own price.

📄

Reports and export

Monthly PDF reports, plus export of your data so it is never locked in.

🔒

Private by design

App lock with a PIN and optional fingerprint or face unlock, and amounts hidden until you reveal them.

🇨🇿

English and Arabic

Full right-to-left layout for Arabic, with reminders and budget alerts that never show amounts on your lock screen.

Your data stays yours

{{site.name}} works without an account. Your accounts and transactions are kept in an encrypted database on your device. Signing in is optional and only needed for cloud features.

If you do use cloud backup, the backup is encrypted on your phone with a password that only you know before it is uploaded.

Delete your {{site.name}} account

Use this page to request permanent deletion of your {{site.name}} account and its cloud data – even if you no longer have the app or cannot sign in on your phone.

What will be deleted

  • Your account (e-mail address and, if you used Google sign-in, the name and profile picture address saved with it).
  • Your encrypted cloud backup.
  • Your plan or subscription record.
  • Your synced records (accounts, transactions, categories, recurring rules, budgets, goals, gold holdings) and any workspace you own.

This cannot be undone. Data stored only on your phone is not affected by this page: it stays in the app until you clear it in Settings or uninstall {{site.name}}. If you want to keep a copy, export your data from the app first.

Request deletion by contacting us

Send the request from the e-mail address of the account, so we can match it to the account. Nothing is deleted until we have confirmed the request comes from the account holder.

  1. Press the button below, or write to {{site.email}} from the account’s e-mail address.
  2. If you cannot use that address any more, tell us how you signed in (e-mail code or Google) and when you last used the app. We will ask only what is needed to verify ownership.
  3. We reply to confirm, delete the account and its cloud data, and tell you when it is done. We aim to finish within 30 days, usually much sooner.

E-mail a deletion request Contact page

Never send your PIN or backup password. We never need them.

Inside the app

Open {{site.name}} → Profile → Delete my account and cloud data.

What may be retained, and why

DataWhat happensWhy
Data on your phoneNot touched by account deletion.It is stored only on your device, under your control. Clear it in Settings or uninstall the app.
Files you exported or sharedStay wherever you saved or sent them.They are outside {{site.name}}.
Records you created inside someone else’s shared workspaceRemain for that workspace’s members, attributed only to an identifier of a deleted user.They belong to the shared workspace. (Shared workspaces are not yet offered in the app.)
A pending workspace invitation sent to your e-mail addressMay remain until the workspace owner removes it.It is stored against the e-mail address, not your account.
Your Google account and Google Play recordsNot affected.They are managed by Google, not {{site.name}}.
E-mails you sent us (support and deletion requests)Kept in our mailbox only as long as needed to handle your request and keep a record of it.To answer you and show that the deletion was carried out.
